Attribution helps businesses understand the source of their customers. CleverTap partners with different attribution providers to track app installations, measure attribution from third-party sources, and display reports on the CleverTap dashboard.

CleverTap supports integrations with popular third-party attribution providers, including:

* [Adjust](doc:adjust)
* [Airbridge](doc:airbridge) 
* [AppsFlyer](doc:appsflyer)
* [Branch](doc:branch)
* [Singular (formerly known as Apsalar)](doc:singular-apsalar)
